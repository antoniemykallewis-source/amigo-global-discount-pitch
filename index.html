What we found / 1 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Show products in use.

The homepage has no product video; Instagram has Reels. Shoppers cannot watch an item here before deciding whether to make the trip. Website check ↗

02

No stock-alert signup.

The homepage promotes arrivals but has no signup. On that page, interested shoppers have no alert option, so new stock may pass them by. Website check ↗

03

Check what ads bring in.

Only Cloudflare analytics appeared on the homepage; no Meta or Google Analytics tag. Unmeasured responses could misguide ad spend. Private reports and server-side tracking were unavailable. Website check ↗

What we found / 2 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Customer trust stays off-page.

The homepage has no customer reviews. Visitors must leave to find the positive Google comments that could help them choose your store. Website check ↗

05

Stock details feel dated.

On October 20, the inventory intro still described September’s snapshot. Shoppers unsure what remains may skip the drive rather than risk a wasted trip. Stock page ↗

06

Search-data format is unused.

The homepage has no JSON-LD business data. Adding this common format would give search engines another clear record of store details. Homepage only; no ranking loss established. Website check ↗

How we would help / 1 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01

Show the stock in action

Film short product demos, add light previews to matching pages and send local ads straight to the featured stock.

02

Build a reason to return

Add an optional new-stock signup and useful arrival updates, with clear consent and an easy way to unsubscribe.

03

Connect ads to useful actions

Review existing reports first. Set up approved measurement for calls, stock enquiries and directions, then compare ads using those actions.

How we would help / 2 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

04

Put real trust on the page

Show a small, honest selection of customer comments with source links. Invite feedback consistently without rewards for positive reviews.

05

Keep arrivals current

Add a checked date, simple staff updates and a clear stock-check route. Remove sold products promptly.

06

Give search clear store facts

Add accurate business details in search-readable code, aligned with the visible address, hours and Google profile. Validate it after release.
